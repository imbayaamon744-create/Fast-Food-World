Who We Are

Fast Food Restaurant is a unique food business owned and operated by Amon and Shanice, who are passionate entrepreneurs. We are dedicated to providing delicious, tasty and affordable samosas to our customers.

Our Story

Fast Food Restaurant was created from a shared passion for good food and a desire to bring tasty and affordable snacks to our community. What started as a simple idea has grown into a business focused on quality, freshness and customer satisfaction.
